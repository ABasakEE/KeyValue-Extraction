Conclusions

The gap is real and depends on layout variety

21.1 points on registration forms, 1.85 on uniform Ad-Buy forms, at matched N = 200.

Almost all errors are field-type errors

Fields that move between templates drop to near 0 F1, and validation on seen templates doesn’t show it.

Fixed spatial graphs overfit layout

Delaunay + Edge-GAT wins on seen layouts but its unseen gap is 43.9 points vs 27.3 for LiLT.

Linking works once decoding is constrained

XFUND link F1 rose from 32 (FUNSD, first model) to 67.2 with curriculum, focal loss and one-to-one decoding.

All problem-statement deliverables are in the repository: template-disjoint splits, models, pipeline, gap measurement, failure analysis and a Gradio interface.
