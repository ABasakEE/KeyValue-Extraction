Before the 21 September review we spent most of our time on the evaluation, because a leaky split would make every later number meaningless. By the review we had a working pipeline on FUNSD and a first seen-versus-unseen gap of 21 points on VRDU. Our FUNSD score was below the published LiLT number, which we said openly at the review. The pilot error taxonomy showed OCR was almost never the problem. From the literature we noticed that entity extraction had been tested under template shift, but key-value linking hadn’t.
