Failure analysis: which fields break on unseen templates

Fields that move collapse

file_date and foreign_principle_name fall to about 0 F1. They sit in a different place on each template, and the model relied on position to find them.

Recognisable fields survive

registration_num keeps 92.8 F1. A registration number looks the same wherever it is printed.

Validation cannot see it

Amendment fold, seed 42: validation F1 93.9, test F1 64.7. Validating on the training templates gave us no sign of the drop.

These are mean per-field F1 scores over nine runs in each setting. The fields that fail move around between templates and don’t have distinctive text. Our pilot error taxonomy agreed: 97.2 percent of unseen-template errors were field-type errors and only 0.2 percent were OCR errors. One thing that surprised us: validation F1 stayed above 90 the whole time while the unseen test sat at 65, so you can’t catch this problem with ordinary validation.
