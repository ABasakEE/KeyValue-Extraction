Four of our seven synthetic form families, with their key-value labels drawn on

IE 643 · Unemployed and Unsupervised · 10 / 18

VRDU registration forms (FARA filings) are our main benchmark because the three templates really are different. Registration documents average 949 words and Ad-Buy documents 1,524, and most run to several pages. The Ad-Buy forms (DeepForm) look much more alike, and that turned out to matter. FUNSD and XFUND (Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese) have key-value link labels, which VRDU doesn’t, so we used them for the linking model. We wrote the synthetic generator ourselves to stress-test the pipeline. All inputs are form images with OCR words and boxes; for VRDU we align the labels to its released OCR tokens by character offsets.
