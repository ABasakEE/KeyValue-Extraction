This is the LiLT baseline, 3 folds times 3 seeds, with N = 200. On seen templates it gets 87.7 F1; on an unseen template, 66.7. How big the gap is depends on how different the templates are. Ad-Buy forms look much alike and barely drop, while the three registration templates are laid out very differently. When we added sliding windows and relative coordinates, the seen score went up to 91.6 but the unseen score stayed around 64.2, so the gap actually grew to 27.3.
