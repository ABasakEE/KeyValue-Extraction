Why LiLT

Keeps layout in its own stream next to a pretrained text encoder, so layout and language are learned separately. Checkpoint: SCUT-DLVCLab/lilt-roberta-en-base.

Why windows

Registration forms average 949 words over several pages. At 512 tokens most words were never seen; overlapping windows cover them all.

Training signal

Cross-entropy on the first sub-word of each word; other sub-words ignored. Coordinate jitter (±80 shift, 0.85–1.15 scale) during training.
