The task: read forms from templates the model has never seen

The input is a scanned form as OCR words and boxes. The output is a set of typed fields such as registration_num or file_date.

Forms from the test templates never enter training, labelled or unlabelled.

MTL (seen): test forms come from templates that are also in training.

UTL (unseen): one whole template is held out for testing.

Gap = mean F1 on MTL minus mean F1 on UTL, at the same training size.

We hold a whole template out and check what happens when the positions change. Both settings train on the same number of documents, so the gap comes from the template change and nothing else.
