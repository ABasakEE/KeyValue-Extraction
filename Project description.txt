
Allotted Project: Cross-Template Key-Value Field Extraction from Scanned Forms: Generalizing to Form Templates With Zero Same-Template Training Data

Project Description: Develop a document understanding framework that extracts key-value fields from scanned forms and generalizes to completely unseen form templates. The model should learn transferable representations of text, layout, spatial relationships and field structure from labeled training templates, while never using any labeled or unlabeled data from the held-out templates during training. The objective is to evaluate how well the proposed representation transfers across substantially different form layouts and identify the structural assumptions that limit generalization.

Expected Deliverables: 1. Multi-Template Form Dataset: Collect and curate scanned forms from multiple genuinely distinct templates with field-level annotations and create a template based split with training templates and at least two completely unseen held-out templates. 2. Cross-Template Field Extraction Model: Develop a representation that jointly captures textual, visual and layout information to extract key-value pairs while promoting generalization across unseen form templates. 3. Zero-Same-Template Generalization Pipeline: Train the model exclusively on the selected training templates and evaluate it on completely unseen templates without using their labeled or unlabeled data during training. 4. Seen vs. Unseen Template Evaluation: Measure and analyze the performance gap to quantify the model's cross-template generalization capability. 5. Failure Analysis: Identify and analyze failure cases on unseen templates, focusing on which layout, structural, textual or spatial assumptions fail and why they affect field extraction.
